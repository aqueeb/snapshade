# Code of conduct

Snapshade is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else the project happens, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- keep criticism about the work, not the person;
- welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently;
- accept that the maintainer decides scope, and disagree without being disagreeable.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove
contributions and block participants who do not follow these rules.

If something happens that you want looked at, contact the maintainer through the email address
on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.
